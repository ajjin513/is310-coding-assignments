The Starry Night

Vincent van Gogh, 1889 — Museum of Modern Art (MoMA)

The Starry Night

According to the MoMA website, Van Gogh painted this in June 1889 while staying at an asylum near Saint-Rémy-de-Provence, France. He could see the hills and cypress tree from his window. He added the village from memory. People often read the swirling sky as a reflection of his state of mind at the time.
